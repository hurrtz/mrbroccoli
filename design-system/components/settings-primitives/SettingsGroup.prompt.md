One settings section in the inset-rows language.

```jsx
<SettingsGroup title="Playback" footer="Replies are read aloud paragraph by paragraph.">
  <SettingsRow icon="play-circle" label="Start speaking" value="As it arrives" last />
</SettingsGroup>
```

Uppercase caption, bordered surface card, and helper prose lives in the footer — never inside the card. Groups stack with their own 14pt top padding; pages are just a column of groups.
