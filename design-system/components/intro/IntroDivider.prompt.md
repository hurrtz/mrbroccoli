Marks a whole step as optional, between the heading and the body.

\`\`\`jsx
<IntroTitle>Let it hear you</IntroTitle>
<IntroDivider label="Optional" />
<IntroBody>This is a voice app, and talking to it is the whole point.</IntroBody>
\`\`\`

Use it only for the two genuinely optional steps. A badge beside the title would read as a status on the step; as a rule it reads as a note about what follows, which is what it is.
