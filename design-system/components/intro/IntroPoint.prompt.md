The tone is the whole message. \`accent\` marks what the app actually requires, \`neutral\` marks what it does not, and \`premium\` marks a paid capability. A step that mixes them is telling the user which parts they can ignore without saying so.

The glyph sits in a hairline round well tinted from the tone, never filled — a filled well at this size reads as a status badge.
