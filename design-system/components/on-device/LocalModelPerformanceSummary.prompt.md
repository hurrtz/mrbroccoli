The two-line verdict under an on-device model.

```jsx
<LocalModelPerformanceSummary evidence="Measured" fit="Viable" details={["14.2 tok/s", "Load 1.8 s", "Headroom 2.4×"]} />
```

Evidence always comes first, then the verdict, then the numbers — the app never states a fit without saying how it knows. Details are joined with a middle dot.
